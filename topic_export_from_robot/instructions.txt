Step 1: 

In your laptop, download `laptop_full_pc2_receiver.py` and place in `/tmp/` folder

open a terminal

export ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY=0
python3 /tmp/laptop_full_pc2_receiver.py


Step 2: 

In robot nos (user@10.21.31.106), download `robot_full_pc2_tcp_sender.py` and place in `/tmp/` folder

open a terminal

sudo bash -lc '
source /opt/robot/scripts/setup_ros2.sh
export FASTRTPS_DEFAULT_PROFILES_FILE=/opt/robot/fastdds.xml
export LAPTOP_IP=10.21.31.100
export PC2_MIN_PERIOD=0.2
python3 /tmp/robot_full_pc2_tcp_sender.py
'

[Above command sends full frames, but at max 5 Hz per topic.]

If you want full speed try `export PC2_MIN_PERIOD=0.0`


Step 3:

Open another terminal in your laptop


export ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY=0
ros2 daemon stop
ros2 topic list --no-daemon -t | grep -Ei "LIDAR|POINTS|TCP"

Expected result:

/LIDAR/POINTS_TCP [sensor_msgs/msg/PointCloud2]
/LIDAR/POINTS2_TCP [sensor_msgs/msg/PointCloud2]

[for testing]
ros2 topic hz /LIDAR/POINTS_TCP
ros2 topic hz /LIDAR/POINTS2_TCP
ros2 topic echo /LIDAR/POINTS_TCP sensor_msgs/msg/PointCloud2 --no-arr
